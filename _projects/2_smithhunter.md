**SmithHunter** is a workflow for the identification of candidate smithRNAs, small RNAs of mitochondrial origin, and their targets from small RNA-seq libraries.

It is described in: Marturano G, **Carli D**, et al. (2024). _SmithHunter: a workflow for the identification of candidate smithRNAs and their targets._ BMC Bioinformatics 25: 286. [doi:10.1186/s12859-024-05909-0](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-024-05909-0)

Its successor, [SmithHunter2]({{ '/projects/1_smithhunter2/' | relative_url }}), is under development.
