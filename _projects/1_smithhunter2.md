**SmithHunter2** finds small RNA loci on every genome present in a sample: nuclear, mitochondrial, symbionts and contaminants.

Each read is assigned to the genome it fits best. Mitochondrial reads are not lost to nuclear copies of mitochondrial DNA (NUMTs), and vice versa.

It is a rewrite of SmithHunter and is no longer limited to mitochondria.
