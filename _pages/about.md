I study how the nuclear and mitochondrial genomes talk to each other. My main interest is small RNAs of mitochondrial origin, called _smithRNAs_: how to find them in sequencing data, how widespread they are across animals, and what they may regulate.

I develop bioinformatic pipelines for small RNA-seq and apply them to questions in evolutionary genomics, from the role of mitochondria in sex determination in bivalves to the possible bacterial origin of these regulatory elements in _Wolbachia_.
